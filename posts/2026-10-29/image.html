A rule with a reason beats a bare rule

Claude can generalize from the explanation.

✗ Bare rule
NEVER use ellipses
✓ With the reason
Your response will be read
aloud by a text-to-speech
engine, so never use ellipses
since the engine will not know
how to pronounce them.

Every "never" needs a "because", or a positive rewrite.
