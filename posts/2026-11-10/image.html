4 rules for tools your agent can use

01 · Scope

Few tools, real workflows

Not one wrapper per endpoint.

02 · Fields

Names, not UUIDs

Readable output, better retrieval.

03 · Size

Cap the response

Claude Code: 25,000 tokens default.

04 · Errors

Say how to fix it

Show a valid input, not a traceback.
