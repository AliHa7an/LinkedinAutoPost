Prompt writing

One sentence gives your prompt a role

Role in the system prompt focuses behavior and tone.

✗ No role
system: (empty)
user: How do I sort a list
      of dictionaries by key?
✓ With a role
system: You are a helpful
  coding assistant
  specializing in Python.
user: How do I sort a list
      of dictionaries by key?

Name the job and audience first, then add the detailed rules.
