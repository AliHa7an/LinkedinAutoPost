4 defenses against prompt injection

01 · Privilege

Give the bot the fewest tools

Less access, less damage.

02 · Code

Check permissions in code

Not in the system prompt.

03 · Human

Approve risky actions

Refunds, deletes, sends.

04 · Content

Treat documents as untrusted

Validate every output.
