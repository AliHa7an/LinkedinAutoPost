4 ways a cron workflow quietly stops

01 · Time

Cron runs in UTC

Set a time zone explicitly if you need one.

02 · Branch

Default branch only

Edits on other branches never run.

03 · Load

Delays at the top of the hour

Use minute 17, not minute 0.

04 · Inactivity

Disabled after 60 days

Public repos with no activity.
