Same date, two different moments

Per MDN, the format decides UTC or local time.

✗ Local time
new Date("2019-01-01T00:00:00")
// parsed in YOUR timezone
✓ UTC
new Date("2019-01-01")
// date-only: parsed as UTC
new Date("2019-01-01T00:00:00Z")
// explicit offset: same moment

Always send an explicit offset for real moments.
